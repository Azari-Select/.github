## About

**Azari Select** is a premium financial-lifestyle platform designed around how members manage money, access services, and move through everyday experiences.

The product brings together financial capabilities, secure account access, membership, curated privileges, partner services, concierge, and digital experiences within a single platform.

Our focus is to make complex financial and lifestyle interactions feel coherent, secure, and dependable across web and mobile experiences.

## What we are building

Azari Select is being developed around a connected set of capabilities including:

- multi-currency account and wallet experiences
- transfers, collections, payouts, and foreign exchange workflows
- transaction history and account management
- membership and access controls
- curated travel, dining, events, and partner privileges
- concierge and support experiences
- offers, rewards, referrals, and partner services
- secure authentication and device-aware session controls
- APIs, webhooks, and integration infrastructure
- administrative, operational, and audit tooling

Availability of specific financial services can depend on eligibility, jurisdiction, compliance requirements, and supported providers.

## Product principles

- **Security first**: authentication, authorization, data protection, and transaction integrity are built into the platform architecture.
- **Clear financial operations**: money movement should be traceable, reconcilable, and resilient to retries or partial failures.
- **Member privacy**: access to financial, identity, and membership information is deliberately scoped and controlled.
- **Reliable experiences**: user-facing flows should remain understandable and recover safely when networks, providers, or dependencies fail.
- **Integrated service**: financial tools, membership, privileges, concierge, and partner experiences should work as one product rather than disconnected features.
- **Operational accountability**: sensitive actions require appropriate auditability, controls, and administrative visibility.

## Platform

Azari Select is built as a multi-surface product spanning web, installable web experiences, and mobile applications, supported by backend services, financial integrations, event processing, notifications, administrative tooling, and partner interfaces.

The platform is designed so that customer-facing experiences and internal operations share the same authorization, audit, eligibility, and transaction rules.

## Engineering

Engineering work across Azari Select includes:
